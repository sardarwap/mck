Refund Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Because OdishaRank.com sells digital content that is delivered instantly through an unlock code, payments are generally non-refundable once the code has been delivered.

We will fix the problem or refund you if:
• your unlock code does not work and we are unable to fix it within 7 days; or
• you paid but did not receive a code within 48 hours.

To request help, send us your payment reference (UTR), the Device ID and a short description on WhatsApp or email. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.
