Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

OdishaRank.com does not require you to create an account.

What we store: your progress, scores, bookmarks and notes are saved only on your own device (in your browser). We do not receive them.

What we receive: when you buy, you send us a payment reference and a Device ID (on WhatsApp or email) so we can create your code. We use this only to deliver your purchase and give support.

We do not sell your information. Payments are processed by your UPI app or payment provider; we do not see your bank or card details.

If you clear your browser data your saved progress will be lost. You can contact us to ask about any information you have shared with us.
